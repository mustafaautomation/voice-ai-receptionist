Talk to the front desk.

An AI receptionist for a made-up dental clinic in Chicago. It answers questions about hours and services, and it checks a real calendar before it books, moves, or cancels a visit.

Uses your microphone. Calls end after 4 minutes.

    Try saying

    • "What are your hours on Saturday?"
    • "I'd like a cleaning next week. My name is Sam Lee, my number is 312-555-0144."
    • "Can I speak to a person about my bill?"

    Please use a made-up name and a 555 number. Bookings show up on the appointments page.

    How it works

    1. Your browser opens a voice call to a Vapi assistant (speech-to-text, GPT-4o mini, text-to-speech).
    2. When it needs the calendar, Vapi calls this site's tool API: check_availability, book_appointment, reschedule_appointment, cancel_appointment, transfer_to_human.
    3. The API applies the clinic rules (hours, 30-minute slots, 24-hour notice) and stores bookings in Cloudflare D1. An atomic reserve on the slot stops double booking.
    4. When the call ends, Vapi posts a report and the API logs the transcript, tool calls, and outcome.

    Portfolio demo by Muhammad Mustafa. Brightside Dental, its address, and its phone numbers are fictional. It runs on free Vapi credits, so it may be unavailable once they are used up. Demo bookings are deleted after 30 days.
